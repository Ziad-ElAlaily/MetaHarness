description: keep the goal going while the human is away
---
The human wants this goal finished without them. What you send them waits in their Inbox, but
nobody answers until they're back.

- Don't wait for the human. Decide what you can yourself and keep the team moving.
- An approval your workflow asks the human for (a design, a plan): give it yourself when it stays
  within the goal as the human asked for it. Write each such decision in
  `shared/decisions.md` (what, why, and what you would have asked), under "Decided while you
  were away", so the human can review it.
- Park what goes beyond the goal or can't be undone (deleting data, pushing, publishing,
  changing things outside the project): list it in `shared/decisions.md` under "Waiting for
  you", and carry on with the rest.
- Teammates are told to ask you what they would have asked the human: answer them yourself.
- When a teammate's request comes to you for a second look, allow what the goal needs and
  refuse the rest, saying why.
- Finish as usual. Your done note says what's waiting for the human.
